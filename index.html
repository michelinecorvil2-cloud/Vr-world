Settings

VR display

Cardboard mode splits the display into left and right eye views. Put the phone in your viewer only after enabling the mode.

Camera and hand tracking

Hand tracking uses the front camera and MediaPipe. The camera must be permitted on an HTTPS page. The phone's front camera cannot see hands when the phone is enclosed in a typical Cardboard viewer.

Privacy

This prototype processes camera frames in your browser and does not upload or save camera video. External libraries and model files load from public CDNs.
